# Research essay — due Thursday 2026-11-05, 23:59

Add one file: `research-essay/<yourname>.html`, lowercase, no spaces.

A standalone, self-contained HTML page on a topic you actually care about, readable on its
own by someone who was not in this class. Use the `research-essay` (idea-to-essay) pipeline:
<https://github.com/chemaoxfz/idea-to-essay>.

It is graded on four things:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| The PCAPS logic is cleanly presented (problem, challenge, approach, proof, significance) | 30 |
| The literature and field context are clear, and the topic is visibly in conversation with them | 25 |
| There is real insight, obtained and analyzed with some depth | 25 |
| The presentation is very clear: tutorial-like, readable by any student in the class, well illustrated | 20 |

Talk to the lecturer about your topic before the middle of October. A badly chosen topic is
the most common reason a strong essay is not possible.
