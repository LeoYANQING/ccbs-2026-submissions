# Session quiz template — CCBS 2026

Filename: `weekN_<name>_quiz.md` — submit together with your digest/outline **before
the lecture** (Wed 23:59), so the TA pair can approve it. The quiz you write tests
**your own 30-minute session's one point**. You are the presenter: you wrote the
material, so writing a good quiz is the last step of your preparation.

---

**Session presenter:** Your name
**Week:** N (date) · **Session point:** [one line: the one thing your session teaches]

**To the author — the bar for the questions:** anyone who actually got the material
should answer in ~30 seconds. Two questions are enough: one multiple choice, one short
fill-in. No trick questions, no trivia, nothing from your private additions that is not
part of the session.
